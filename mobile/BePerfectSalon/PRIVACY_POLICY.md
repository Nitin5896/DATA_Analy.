## Information We Collect

When you create an account or book an appointment, the App collects:

- **Account information**: your name, email address, and phone number, which you provide
  when signing up.
- **Booking information**: the services you select, your chosen date, time, and stylist
  preference, and any notes you type when booking an appointment.

We do not collect your device's location, contacts, camera, microphone, or photos. The App
does not include advertising or analytics tracking SDKs.

## How We Use Information

- To create and manage your account.
- To create, display, and let you cancel your appointment bookings.
- To let the salon staff see and prepare for upcoming appointments.

We do not sell your information or share it with third parties for advertising or marketing.

## Where Information Is Stored

Account and booking data is stored using Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud
Firestore), a cloud infrastructure provider. Firebase processes this data on our behalf under
its own security and privacy commitments; see
[Google's Firebase privacy and security page](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
for details. We do not use any other third-party data processor.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Your booking history is kept so the salon can reference past appointments and so you can see
your own booking history in the App. You can permanently delete your account and all
associated data (profile and bookings) at any time from **Profile → Delete My Account** in
the App. This action is immediate and cannot be undone.

## Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information
from children under 13.
